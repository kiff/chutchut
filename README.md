# chutchut

**In design: nothing runs yet.**

An inbox for AI agents, on your own domain. Mail arrives at a door: a paired sender goes to an agent that triages and drafts, anyone else knocks and waits for you, a send waits for your approval unless you've allowed that kind of reply, and everything leaves a record.
