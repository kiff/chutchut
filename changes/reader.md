- **Mail is read only once it's scanned clean, and a paired sender's mail is kept.** When GuardDuty finds no threat in a paired sender's message, it's copied out of the 30-day area and kept until the owner deletes it. Malware is refused and loses its sender's address, and a scan that didn't finish is held for the owner. Mail still waiting for its scan after three days is raised, so none expires unseen. The day's mail cap counts for each address of ours (a catch-all domain as one) and for the whole account; the knock caps count across all our addresses, so writing to many addresses of ours gives no fresh room.
