- **The functions on AWS, as code** (`deploy/aws.sh up`): the webhook behind a function URL subscribed to the topic over HTTPS, the reader sent GuardDuty's scan result by an EventBridge rule, and a daily check that raises mail still waiting for its scan on an alerts topic (optionally mailed). Each function's role may do only what it does; logs are kept 30 days. Applied by its owner, never by CI.
