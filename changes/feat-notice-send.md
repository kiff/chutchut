- **tafs can send a notice: its own mail to one address, answering no message.** It's the copy of a door's answer to the account's owner, or an answer report to support. tafs asks for it by name (`notice: true`) with its read key. It goes to one address, from one of the addresses chutchut receives for, never threaded, once per idempotency key, and under its own day's cap for the account (`CHUTCHUT_NOTICES_PER_DAY`, 50 by default). Everything else is still only a reply to a message that came here, to its writer.
