- **tafs can send a notice: its own mail to one address, answering no message.** It's the copy of a door's answer to the account's owner, or an answer report to support. tafs asks for it by name (`notice: true`) with its read key. It goes to one address, from one of the addresses chutchut receives for, never threaded, once per idempotency key, and under its own day's caps: one in all, every account's together (`CHUTCHUT_NOTICES_PER_DAY`, 5,000 by default), and one for each address (`CHUTCHUT_NOTICES_PER_RECIPIENT`, 100 by default). Everything else is still only a reply to a message that came here, to its writer.
